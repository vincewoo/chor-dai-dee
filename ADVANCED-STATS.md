1. Core Performance Metrics

Win/Placement Tracking:

    Win Rate - percentage of games won vs. total games played

    Placement Distribution - 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th place finishes and frequency

    Consistent Finishing Position - identify if players typically finish high or low

    Head-to-Head Records - track performance specifically against each opponent

Scoring Efficiency:

    Penalty Points Per Round - average cards left unplayed per hand (Big 2's losing point system)

    Cards Shed Efficiency - average number of plays needed to shed your hand

    Quick Elimination Rate - percentage of rounds where player sheds all cards first

    Penalty Severity - track if player frequently gets stuck with 10+ cards (doubled penalty) or 13 cards (tripled penalty)

2. Strategic Play Metrics

Card Control & Decision Making:

    Lead Success Rate - how often a player successfully leads piles and maintains control

    Play Aggressiveness - number of combinations played per game vs. passing frequency

    Pass Rate - percentage of times unable to beat the current pile (reveals hand strength issues)

    Combination Type Usage - frequency of singles, pairs, 5-card hands, etc.

    High Card Play Timing - when do players deploy their strongest cards (early vs. late)?

Card Counting Capability (Unique to Big 2):

    Play Predictability - if tracking cards played, measure how well players avoid cards likely held by others

    Memory Effectiveness - correlate player win rate with cards-remaining accuracy

    Deck Awareness Score - track if player decisions improve as deck depletes

3. Comparative & Competitive Metrics

ELO-Style Ranking System (adapted for multiplayer):

    Dynamic rating adjusted by:

        Placement in game (1st/2nd/3rd/4th)

        Opponent strength (higher-rated opponents = bigger rating swing)

        Score differential (4-point spread beats 1-point spread differently)

    Similar to poker's bankroll tracking systems

Player Segmentation:

    Skill Tiers - Aggressive, Conservative, Balanced playing styles

    Risk Profile - tendency to take risky plays vs. safe plays

    Adaptability - performance variance across different opponent combinations

4. Session & Engagement Metrics

    Games Per Session - how many rounds/games in a sitting

    Win Streaks - consecutive game wins

    Performance vs. Fatigue - does win rate decline over longer sessions?

    Participation Frequency - how often players return to play

5. Advanced Analytics (Optional)

Decision Efficiency Score:

    Measure correct plays vs. suboptimal plays (if you have game logic to evaluate hand strength)

    Similar to poker's "decision points per hand"

Variance Tracking:

    How much does a player's performance swing (high variance vs. stable)?

    Identify lucky vs. skilled wins

Player Matchup Analysis:

    Some players might consistently beat certain opponents

    Create player compatibility metrics
